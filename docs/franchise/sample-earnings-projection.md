**For:** signing-day conversation with prospective Full Franchise instructor

**Tier modelled:** Full Franchise (£195/week Weekly Fee, £250 vehicle deposit deducted from Week 1 — see note at end)
**Content-Licensing Arrangement:** active (Schedule E in force — £35/week Content Facilitation Fee netted against the Weekly Fee; learners with social-media consent receive 5% discount on lessons absorbed by CCL)

---

## Read this first

This document shows what your **first 12 weeks** of operating with Coach Carter Ltd realistically look like, in three scenarios:

- **Best case** — strong start, fully booked by week 8, no holidays/sickness
- **Expected case** — realistic ramp, fully booked by week 10, one quiet week
- **Worst case** — slow ramp still unfinished at week 12, sickness, one weather week

These aren't promises. They're the honest range of what could happen, based on:
- £55/hour standard lesson rate
- Most lessons 90 minutes (£82.50/lesson)
- 18 lessons/week as the steady-state target once you're booked up
- Weekly Fee £195/week — minus £35/week Content Facilitation Fee = **Effective Weekly Fee £160/week**
- Stripe processing fees roughly £1.44 per 90-min lesson, **passed through at cost** (CCL retains zero margin on Stripe fees per agreement clause 4.6)
- Week 1 vehicle deposit of £250 deducted from week 1 calculation per agreement clause 5.5

Numbers are gross — ignore your personal tax (sole-trader National Insurance + income tax on the net amounts shown).

Don't undercut yourself early to win learners — the platform has the trial-lesson mechanism for that.

## What this projection does NOT include

So you can't be ambushed:

- **Personal tax.** Sole-trader Class 2/4 NI + income tax on the net amounts shown. Rough rule of thumb: keep aside 25–30% of net earnings for HMRC.
- **Vehicle running costs that are NOT in your Weekly Fee.** Fuel is on you. Charging if hybrid/EV is on you. Personal cleaning/valeting is on you.
- **The first month before Start Date.** During vehicle-prep month (4 weeks before Start Date), no Weekly Fee, no payouts, no income from CCL.
- **Bulk-tier opt-in.** If you opt in to Bulk Tiers (Schedule Part B), you absorb 2.5%/5%/7.5% off bulk-credit purchases. This projection assumes you've **opted out**, which is the safer default for year 1. You can opt in later.
- **Your Insurance.** Tuition vehicle insurance, public liability, professional indemnity — all yours per clause 7.3. Budget roughly £1,200–1,800/year for tuition cover (varies by provider, ADI/PDI status, claims history).
- **Late-cancellation revenue.** Lessons cancelled within 48 hours of start are paid out as if delivered (clause 4.10). This projection doesn't model them — they'll happen and they'll add to your figures.
- **Lead Floor rebate.** The worst-case £84.50 quarterly rebate falls in *the next* quarter (weeks 14–26), so it doesn't appear in the 12-week table.

## The conversation this projection is for

Three things to actually talk through on signing day:

**1. Pick the scenario you genuinely think describes your first 12 weeks.** Not best, not worst — the one you'd bet on for yourself. That number is what you should plan your personal cashflow against. CCL plans against the same number for our side.

**2. Look at week 1 specifically.** Net £0 from CCL in week 1 of worst case (and net just −£85.76 in best case before recovery). Make sure you have personal savings to cover roughly 4 weeks of living expenses *before* you start, because there's no income from CCL in your first month-or-so on the road.

**3. The shortfall mechanic is automatic.** You won't get an invoice from us when revenue is below the Effective Fee. It just rolls forward and recovers from your next positive week. You can see your running shortfall balance on `/instructor/earnings` at all times. **No surprises.**
