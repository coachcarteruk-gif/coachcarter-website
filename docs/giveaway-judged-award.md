## Two proposed judging questions

Owner has now approved both criteria below, following the equal-weight proposal.
Owner prefers to conduct reviews himself because of time constraints. Record that
preference without treating independent final judging as agreed or unnecessary.
Fraser can carry out initial reading/assessment; independent participation in
the final subjective decision remains a release issue under CAP 8.26 if applicable.
No judge has been appointed and no applications have been assessed by this agent.

1. **What difference would being able to drive make to their everyday life?**
   Examples might include work, education, caring responsibilities or independence.
   These are examples, not a requirement to be employed or have children.
2. **What is currently making it difficult for them to learn?**
   Consider the practical or financial barriers the offered lessons could address.
   No requirement to disclose a diagnosis, trauma, benefits records or bank details.

Proposed equal importance for these two questions. The person and nominator can
describe circumstances in ordinary language; eloquence, length, spelling, marketing
opt-ins, filming willingness, employment category and number of nominations must
not increase the assessment. Provide the same opportunity to clarify missing facts.
Do not treat unanswered optional/private questions as evidence of lesser need.

Fraser and a competent independent panel member each review the same relevant
material, note how it relates to the two criteria, then record a reasoned joint
decision. The independent member must have a genuine role, not simply approve
Fraser's choice. Agree conflict-of-interest handling and access/confidentiality
before sharing any story. Judge names must be available on request if CAP applies.

Proposed tie resolution: jointly reconsider the two criteria using consistent
clarifications; if still tied, obtain a further independent assessment against the
same criteria. Do not fall back to an unannounced coin toss or personal preference.

Proposed replacement: agree and privately record a ranked reserve using the same
criteria when choosing the recipient. After the existing 120-hour response window,
Fraser manually records non-response and offers the award to the next eligible
reserve. This replaces, rather than activates, the old random-redraw proposal.
Owner approval is still needed. No automated selection, messages or timers.
