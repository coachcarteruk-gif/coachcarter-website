## 2. Definitions

- **"Agreement"** — this document plus the signed Schedule plus any signed written amendments.
- **"Platform"** — the Coach Carter Ltd booking, payments and learner-management system at coachcarter.uk and any successor domains.
- **"Initial Term"** — 12 months from the Start Date.
- **"Term"** — the Initial Term and each subsequent 12-month renewal period under clause 5.2 during which this Agreement remains in force. References to "the Term" mean the period currently in force.
- **"Start Date"** — calculated per clause 5.1.
- **"Weekly Fee"** — the per-instructor fee owed to Coach Carter Ltd, set in the Schedule.
- **"Tier"** — Full Franchise or Part Franchise, defined in clause 3.
- **"Default Rate"** — the standard hourly tuition rate set by Coach Carter Ltd as published on the Platform from time to time. The Default Rate **as at the Start Date** is recorded on the Schedule.
- **"Qualifying Lead"** — a learner whom Coach Carter Ltd routes to the Instructor via the Platform, the free-trial signup flow, or by direct introduction. Whether the Instructor accepts or declines a routed lead does not affect whether it counts as Qualifying — Coach Carter Ltd has done its part by routing. **A routed lead does NOT count as Qualifying** if the learner subsequently no-shows, cancels before any lesson is delivered, or reschedules to a different instructor before any lesson is delivered with the original Instructor.
- **"Schedule"** — the per-instructor terms sheet attached to and forming part of this Agreement.

## 4. Fees and payouts

### 4.1 Weekly Fee

The Weekly Fee is recorded in the Schedule. Standard tier fees as of May 2026:

- **Full Franchise**: £195/week (placeholder — sense-checked against vehicle purchase quote, insurance not relevant since instructor-borne, plus servicing/MOT/breakdown reserves).
- **Part Franchise**: £70/week.

The Weekly Fee is owed for **every week of the Term regardless of whether the Instructor delivers any lessons**. There is no contractual sick pay, no contractual holiday allowance, and no minimum-earnings guarantee, except as set out in clause 4.4 (Lead Floor).

### 4.2 Payout mechanic

Each week, the Instructor's payout = lesson revenue − Weekly Fee − Stripe processing fees (clause 4.6).

- If positive, Coach Carter Ltd pays the Instructor the difference.
- If negative, the shortfall rolls forward and is deducted from the following week's payout. The shortfall continues to roll until cleared. *[Internal note: if a shortfall is still rolling after 4 weeks, Coach Carter Ltd will reach out for a conversation rather than continue silent deductions — soft practice, not a contractual term.]*

The shortfall must be cleared either by future positive payouts or by the Instructor making payment by bank transfer or Stripe Payment Link. **Year one practice** (informal, not a contractual commitment): shortfall conversations are handled personally by Fraser before any formal recovery step. *[NOTE FOR SOLICITOR: this informal year-one practice is deliberately not contractualised — no automated invoicing, no Bacs DD. Confirm this is workable.]*

**Settlement of rolling shortfall on termination.** If a rolling shortfall remains at the date of termination (whether by Coach Carter Ltd under clause 5.3 or by the Instructor under clause 5.4), the residual is settled within **30 days of the termination date** as follows:

- Any deposit held under clause 5.5 is credited first.
- Any final positive payout owed to the Instructor is credited second.
- Any remaining residual is an ordinary debt due from the Instructor to Coach Carter Ltd, payable by bank transfer.
- If the residual after credit of deposit and final payout is **less than £100**, it is written off (de minimis).

*[NOTE FOR SOLICITOR: please confirm this post-termination settlement mechanic is enforceable in the form drafted — particularly the de minimis write-off and the order of set-off against the deposit.]*

### 4.3 Payout schedule

Payouts run **every Friday between 14:00 and 16:00**, subject to technical logistics. Each payout includes all lessons completed in the 7 days preceding the cut-off (cut-off: 12:00 noon on payout Friday).

### 4.4 Lead Floor

Coach Carter Ltd commits to routing a minimum of **0.5 Qualifying Leads per Week** to the Instructor (or the figure on the Schedule, if different), measured over each consecutive 13-Week period beginning on the Start Date. The "Leads Owed" for a 13-Week period is the per-week figure × 13, rounded to the nearest whole lead (default 0.5 × 13 = 7).

If at the end of any 13-Week period Coach Carter Ltd has routed fewer than the Leads Owed, the Weekly Fee for the **following 13-Week period** is reduced by a per-week amount calculated as:

> **Reduction per week = Maximum Reduction × (Leads Short ÷ Leads Owed), rounded up to the nearest 50p.**

The **Maximum Reduction** is set on the Schedule (default **£15.00/week**). It is the reduction applied when Coach Carter Ltd routes zero Qualifying Leads during the 13-Week period; smaller shortfalls produce proportionally smaller reductions. The Maximum Reduction does not scale with the Lead Floor figure — even if a higher Lead Floor is set, £15 (or the figure on the Schedule) remains the maximum per-week reduction.

The reduction does not roll forward beyond one 13-Week period.

**Worked examples** (Lead Floor 0.5/week, default £15 Maximum Reduction):

| Leads delivered | Leads short | Reduction per week (rounded up to 50p) | Quarterly relief |
|---|---|---|---|
| 7+ | 0 | £0.00 | £0.00 |
| 6 | 1 | £2.50 | £32.50 |
| 5 | 2 | £4.50 | £58.50 |
| 4 | 3 | £6.50 | £84.50 |
| 3 | 4 | £9.00 | £117.00 |
| 2 | 5 | £11.00 | £143.00 |
| 1 | 6 | £13.00 | £169.00 |
| 0 | 7 | £15.00 | £195.00 |

If quarter 1 fee was £195/week and 5 Qualifying Leads were delivered (2 short), quarter 2 fee becomes £190.50/week. If quarter 2 then delivers 9 Qualifying Leads, quarter 3 returns to the full Weekly Fee.

### 4.5 Fee changes

Coach Carter Ltd may give written notice during the **final three months of the Term** proposing a revised Weekly Fee for the renewal Term. Coach Carter Ltd will aim to issue any such proposal as early in that 3-month window as practicable so the Instructor has reasonable time to evaluate.

If the Instructor does not accept the proposed fee, the Instructor may decline renewal at end-of-Term without penalty per clause 5.2.

**Late-proposal protection.** If Coach Carter Ltd issues a fee-change proposal under this clause **fewer than 60 days before the end of the Term**, the Instructor's deadline to give renewal-decline notice under clause 5.2 is **automatically extended to 30 days after the proposal is delivered**, even if that date falls after the original notice deadline. If the extended deadline falls after the original end-of-Term, the Term is automatically extended at the **original (unrevised) Weekly Fee** until that revised deadline. This ensures the Instructor always has at least 30 days to consider a fee-change proposal before being deemed to have auto-renewed.

The Weekly Fee **cannot be changed within a live Term** except by signed written amendment to the Schedule.

### 4.6 Stripe processing fees

Stripe processing fees (typically 1.5% + 20p per UK card transaction) are deducted from the lesson revenue figure used to calculate the Instructor's payout. **Coach Carter Ltd does not retain or add any margin on these fees** — they are passed through at cost. The actual fee deducted is itemised on each weekly payout statement.

### 4.7 Hourly rate

The Default Rate at the Start Date is recorded on the Schedule. The Instructor's actual hourly rate is also recorded on the Schedule and may be set higher or lower than the Default Rate by mutual agreement at signing.

Coach Carter Ltd may revise the Default Rate from time to time on not less than 30 days' written notice. **Revisions to the Instructor's actual rate require mutual agreement and a signed amendment to the Schedule**.

### 4.8 Bulk-tier opt-in

The Platform offers learners optional bulk credit packages (e.g. 12-hour, 24-hour, 36-hour packages at percentage discounts published on the Platform from time to time). As of May 2026: 12h at 2.5% off, 24h at 5% off, 36h at 7.5% off. Percentages and thresholds may change.

The Instructor opts in or out of bulk packages on the Schedule. **If opted in, the Instructor absorbs the discount on the relevant purchases** — the Instructor's effective hourly rate for that bulk purchase is reduced accordingly, and their payout reflects the reduced rate.

The Instructor may change their opt-in status at any time by written notice (taking effect from the following week). **In-flight credits already purchased keep their original effective rate** — opt-out only affects future purchases.

### 4.9 Per-learner custom rates

The Instructor may agree a different hourly rate with a specific learner via the Platform's per-learner rate function. Such a rate overrides both the Default Rate and the Instructor's actual rate **for that learner only**, until withdrawn or amended.

### 4.10 Late cancellations

Learners may cancel up to **48 hours before lesson start** for a full refund. Cancellations made within 48 hours of lesson start forfeit the lesson; **the Instructor is paid for the lesson as if delivered**. Cancellations made more than 48 hours before lesson start mean the Instructor receives no payment for that lesson.

**Pricing of a forfeited lesson.** A lesson cancelled within 48 hours of start time pays out at the **same effective rate at which the underlying credit was originally purchased** (so a credit purchased under a bulk discount pays out at the discounted effective rate, not at the Default Rate). Stripe processing fees attributable to that credit are deducted from the payout in the same proportion they would have been deducted on a delivered lesson. The forfeited lesson is treated identically to a delivered lesson for payout-calculation purposes.

### 4.11 Content-Licensing Arrangement (optional)

If the Instructor and Coach Carter Ltd have entered into the **Content-Licensing Arrangement** in Schedule Part E, the Content Facilitation Fee defined therein is paid weekly to the Instructor by net set-off against the Weekly Fee, and is itemised separately on the weekly payout statement. This Arrangement is governed entirely by Schedule Part E and is **separately terminable** from this Agreement on the notice provisions there. Termination of Schedule Part E does not affect any other clause of this Agreement; termination of this Agreement automatically terminates Schedule Part E.

The Content-Licensing Arrangement is independent of and additional to all other fee provisions in this clause 4. It does not vary the Weekly Fee, the Default Rate, the Instructor's hourly rate, the bulk-tier opt-in, or any other commercial term.

## 5. Term, renewal, termination

### 5.1 Start Date

- For Instructors holding a **Trainee Licence (PDI pink licence)**: the Start Date is the **first Monday that falls at least 6 clear days after** the Trainee Licence arrives.
- For Instructors holding a **full ADI green badge**: the Start Date is mutually agreed in writing, allowing Coach Carter Ltd time to source a vehicle (Full Franchise only), fit dual-control, and apply decals.

### 5.2 Term and renewal

The initial Term is **12 months from the Start Date**.

The Term auto-renews for successive 12-month periods unless either party gives **written notice not less than 30 days before the end of the current Term**. A renewal may be on revised commercial terms if proposed under clause 4.5 and accepted; otherwise it is on the same terms.

### 5.3 Termination by Coach Carter Ltd

Termination grounds are tiered. **Material breaches** entitle Coach Carter Ltd to terminate immediately on written notice. **Other breaches** require a written warning and a 14-day opportunity to cure before termination.

**Material breaches** (immediate termination on written notice):

- The Instructor loses or fails to maintain valid ADI registration or Trainee Licence.
- Required insurance under clause 7.2 lapses for more than 14 days without reinstatement.
- The Instructor's DBS check fails.
- Teaching while under the influence of alcohol or drugs (clause 6.3).
- Entering a personal, romantic, or sexual relationship with a learner (clause 6.3).
- The Instructor commits a criminal offence, supported by either prosecution, conviction, formal caution, or written admission by the Instructor, that brings the Coach Carter brand into disrepute.
- Unpaid rolling shortfall remaining unpaid more than 30 days after written demand from Coach Carter Ltd.
- The Instructor becomes insolvent or bankrupt.

**Other breaches** (warning + 14-day cure period; second instance within 12 months → immediate termination):

- DBS check expiring without timely renewal.
- Failure to comply with the DVSA Code of Practice for ADIs (excluding matters that already fall under "Material breaches").
- Lack of reasonable punctuality or professional presentation.
- Social-media or public conduct bringing the Coach Carter brand into disrepute (excluding criminal conduct, which is a Material breach).
- Any other breach of this Agreement that is not specifically listed as a Material breach.

For the avoidance of doubt: Coach Carter Ltd has **no without-cause termination right**. Coach Carter Ltd cannot end the Agreement at will.

**Precautionary suspension on credible safeguarding or brand-harm allegation.** If the Instructor is **arrested, charged, or otherwise the subject of a credible allegation** in connection with an offence that Coach Carter Ltd reasonably believes (i) raises a safeguarding concern in relation to learners, or (ii) is likely to bring the Coach Carter brand into disrepute, Coach Carter Ltd may **suspend the Instructor with immediate written notice** pending resolution of the matter. During suspension:

- The Instructor must not deliver any lessons or hold themselves out as a Coach Carter instructor.
- Payouts for any lessons delivered after the suspension date are forfeited.
- The Weekly Fee continues to accrue.
- The Lead Floor (clause 4.4) does not apply during the suspension period (i.e. CCL is not penalised for failing to route leads to a suspended instructor).
- If the matter resolves without prosecution, conviction, formal caution, or written admission within a reasonable period, the suspension is lifted and the Instructor returns to active status. Accrued Weekly Fees during suspension remain owed; lessons forfeited during suspension remain forfeited.
- If the matter resolves into a Material breach trigger (prosecution, conviction, formal caution, written admission) per the list above, suspension converts to termination on the date that resolution becomes known to Coach Carter Ltd.

This is a **precautionary measure, not a finding of guilt**, and is intended to protect learners and the Coach Carter brand while the underlying matter is investigated by the proper authorities.

### 5.4 Termination by the Instructor

The Instructor may terminate by giving **3 months' written notice** at any time. During the 3-month notice period:

- The Instructor continues paying the Weekly Fee.
- The Instructor continues delivering lessons booked through the Platform.
- The Lead Floor (clause 4.4) continues to apply.

After the 3-month notice period ends, no further Weekly Fees are owed. The Instructor pays the decal-removal fee per clause 3.3.

### 5.5 Post-termination — vehicle (Full Franchise only)

Within 7 days of termination, the Instructor must return the vehicle to Coach Carter Ltd in **roadworthy condition, fair wear and tear excepted**. Damage beyond fair wear and tear is settled via the Instructor's insurance.

A **deposit of £250** is collected at Start Date. The deposit is taken by deducting £250 from the Instructor's **week 1 payout calculation**, regardless of whether that calculation is otherwise positive or negative:

- If week 1 payout would otherwise be positive by £250 or more, the deposit is deducted and the remainder is paid out.
- If week 1 payout would otherwise be positive by less than £250, the deposit is deducted and the difference becomes part of the rolling shortfall under clause 4.2.
- If week 1 payout would otherwise be negative, the deposit is added to the rolling shortfall (deepening it by £250).

This guarantees the deposit is always collected, regardless of the Instructor's first-week earnings, so it is available as a buffer against vehicle damage throughout the Term.

The deposit is returned within **14 days of vehicle return**, less any deductions for damage beyond fair wear and tear, itemised in writing. The order of credits on termination is set out in clause 4.2 (deposit credited before final payout, before any residual shortfall becomes an ordinary debt).

### 5.6 Post-termination — learners

Learners introduced to the Instructor via Coach Carter Ltd are **free to follow the Instructor or to remain with Coach Carter Ltd**, at the learner's choice.

The Instructor must not export, copy or retain the Coach Carter Ltd learner list (full database of learners) on termination. Any contact with the Instructor's former learners must rely on those learners reaching out independently or remembering the Instructor by name — **the Instructor cannot use the platform's learner data to solicit them**.

This clause survives termination.

## 6. Operations and conduct

### 6.1 Booking system use

The Instructor is **encouraged but not required** to take all Coach Carter Ltd-routed bookings via the Platform. The Instructor may take learners off-platform if they prefer.

The Instructor acknowledges that taking Coach Carter Ltd-introduced learners off-platform means **Coach Carter Ltd cannot provide the booking record, progress tracking, or dispute support** for those off-platform lessons.

The Instructor may also teach independently, under their own name, with their own learners, alongside this Agreement. There is no exclusivity.

### 6.2 Decals — required for the duration of the Term

Coach Carter-branded decals are fitted at the start of the Term (clauses 3.1 / 3.2) and must remain displayed on the tuition vehicle **for the duration of the Term**. The Instructor must not remove, alter, cover, deface, or modify the decals.

Where the Instructor delivers tuition to their own independent learners (clause 6.4) using the same vehicle, the Instructor must disclose to those learners that they franchise with Coach Carter Ltd, so the brand presence on the vehicle is not misleading.

**Damage to decals.** The Instructor must report any damage, peeling, fading, or other deterioration of the decals to Coach Carter Ltd within **24 hours of discovery**. Coach Carter Ltd will decide, at its discretion, whether to repair or replace the decals at Coach Carter Ltd's cost in the interests of brand presentation. Damage caused by the Instructor's negligence (e.g. solvent contact, washing technique that strips vinyl) may be charged to the Instructor; damage from ordinary use, weather, or third-party causes is at Coach Carter Ltd's cost.

### 6.3 Conduct standards

The Instructor will at all times during the Term:

- Maintain valid ADI/PDI registration with the DVSA.
- Maintain a valid DBS check, renewed at the Instructor's cost.
- Comply with the **DVSA Code of Practice for ADIs**.
- Not teach while under the influence of alcohol or drugs.
- Not enter any personal, romantic or sexual relationship with a learner during the teaching relationship and **for 12 months after the teaching relationship ends**.
- Be reasonably punctual and professionally presented.
- Not publish any content (social media, public statements) that brings the Coach Carter brand into disrepute.

### 6.4 Independent marketing

The Instructor may run their own marketing **under their own name**, for their own independent learners, without restriction. The Instructor may **not** run paid marketing under the Coach Carter brand without prior written approval from Coach Carter Ltd.

### 6.5 Vehicle use (Full Franchise only)

The Instructor may use the Coach Carter Ltd-supplied vehicle for **personal use** in addition to tuition, subject to:

- All conduct restrictions in this Agreement applying to all use of the vehicle (including criminal-conduct, drink/drug-driving, behaviour bringing the brand into disrepute).
- **No smoking** in the vehicle at any time, by anyone, including learners and personal-use passengers. Smoking enforcement is set out in clause 6.5.1.
- Eating and drinking permitted, subject to clause 6.5.2 on interior condition.
- Daily checks (oil, tyres, washer fluid, lights) by the Instructor.
- Any fault, damage, or incident reported to Coach Carter Ltd within **24 hours**.
- No modifications to the vehicle and no removal of decals or dual-control kit by the Instructor.
- Servicing and MOT scheduled by Coach Carter Ltd; the Instructor must release the vehicle when scheduled. Servicing and MOT days are typically used for in-person check-in meetings between the Instructor and Coach Carter Ltd.

### 6.5.1 Smoking — enforcement

Smoking in the vehicle is prohibited. Enforcement is reactive: Coach Carter Ltd does not routinely inspect the vehicle, but acts on credible reports (typically from learners) or evidence at servicing / MOT / handover.

- **First reported / detected instance** of smoking in the vehicle: **written warning** under the Other breach mechanic in clause 5.3 (warning + 14-day cure period; cure means committing in writing not to repeat and, if necessary, paying for any required ozone treatment to remove existing odour).
- **Second instance within 12 months** of the first: **Material breach** under clause 5.3 (immediate termination).
- **At handover (regardless of warning history)**: if the vehicle smells of smoke or shows physical evidence of smoking, the cost of ozone treatment and any other remediation required to neutralise the odour is charged to the Instructor, set off against the deposit (clause 5.5), with any balance billable.

### 6.5.2 Interior condition — beyond ordinary valeting

Eating and drinking are permitted, but the Instructor is responsible for keeping the interior in reasonable condition during the Term and at handover. The cost of cleaning the vehicle interior **beyond what would be required after ordinary tuition use** is at the Instructor's cost. Examples include:

- Stain removal (food, drink, dye).
- Deep clean of upholstery.
- Removal of persistent odours (food, fragrance, pet, smoke per clause 6.5.1).
- Removal of pet hair.
- Removal of ground-in debris.

Such costs are charged at the actual invoiced cost of the cleaning, set off against the deposit (clause 5.5) at termination, with any balance billable. Routine valeting after ordinary tuition use is at Coach Carter Ltd's cost and is not chargeable to the Instructor.

## 7. Status, tax, insurance

### 7.1 Self-employment

The Instructor is engaged as an **independent contractor and self-employed franchisee**. Nothing in this Agreement constitutes an employer-employee relationship, a partnership, or an agency.

The Instructor is responsible for their own income tax, National Insurance contributions, and (if applicable) VAT registration.

For the avoidance of doubt:
- The Instructor sets their own working hours and availability.
- The Instructor may refuse any specific booking.
- The Instructor may engage in other ADI work (independent or with another franchisor).
- The Instructor bears real financial risk via the Weekly Fee, owed regardless of work delivered (subject to clause 4.4).
- The Instructor's services are personal and may not be substituted (the ADI licence is personal — industry-standard limitation).

### 7.2 Insurance — mandatory

The Instructor must hold and maintain throughout the Term:

- **Tuition vehicle insurance** (Full: covering the Coach Carter Ltd vehicle, with Coach Carter Ltd named as registered keeper / loss payee. Part: covering the Instructor's own vehicle, with full tuition use cover).
- **Public liability insurance** of at least **£5 million**.
- **Professional indemnity insurance** of at least **£1 million**.

A common and recommended way to obtain Public Liability and Professional Indemnity insurance is through **ADI association membership** (e.g. ADINJC, MSA GB), which typically bundles both at ~£100–150/year. The Instructor may obtain these insurances by any other route, provided the cover meets the minimums above.

### 7.3 Proof of insurance, lapse handling, and uninsured-teaching indemnity

#### 7.3.1 Proof of insurance

The Instructor must provide certificates of insurance to Coach Carter Ltd:

- **Before the Start Date** (no Start Date can occur until valid certificates for all required insurances are received and approved by Coach Carter Ltd).
- **On each policy renewal**, by emailing the new certificate to Coach Carter Ltd within **7 days of the renewal date**. Failure to provide the renewal certificate within this window is itself a breach (an Other breach under clause 5.3, curable on production of the certificate).
- **On request** at any time.

#### 7.3.2 Tuition vehicle insurance — strict

Tuition vehicle insurance is **safety-critical**. Driving instruction without it is illegal. Accordingly:

- A lesson is only **eligible for payout** if Coach Carter Ltd has approved the Instructor's tuition vehicle insurance as valid for the date that lesson is delivered. Any lesson delivered while tuition vehicle insurance is lapsed is **not eligible for payout**, and the Instructor receives no revenue for that lesson.
- The Instructor may continue to take *bookings* for future dates while waiting for insurance to be approved (e.g. during onboarding), but cannot deliver any lesson, or earn payout from any lesson, until tuition vehicle insurance is in place and approved.
- A lapse of tuition vehicle insurance of more than **14 days** is a **Material breach** under clause 5.3 entitling Coach Carter Ltd to terminate immediately.
- The Weekly Fee **continues to accrue during any tuition-vehicle-insurance lapse**.

There is no cure period for tuition vehicle insurance lapses — the gap in payout eligibility is the only consequence for short lapses, and the Material-breach termination right kicks in at 14 days.

#### 7.3.3 Public Liability and Professional Indemnity insurance — cure period

Public Liability and Professional Indemnity insurance are **administratively required** but not safety-critical to a single lesson. Accordingly:

- If Coach Carter Ltd discovers a lapse of either Public Liability or Professional Indemnity insurance, Coach Carter Ltd will give **written notice** to the Instructor.
- The Instructor has **7 days from the date of written notice** to reinstate the lapsed cover and provide a new certificate.
- During the 7-day cure period, the Instructor **may continue to teach and remains eligible for payouts**.
- If the Instructor fails to reinstate cover within 7 days of written notice, Coach Carter Ltd may suspend payouts until cover is reinstated. The Weekly Fee continues to accrue.
- A lapse of more than **14 days** uncured is a **Material breach** under clause 5.3 entitling Coach Carter Ltd to terminate immediately.

#### 7.3.4 Uninsured-teaching indemnity

The Instructor **indemnifies Coach Carter Ltd against any loss, claim, third-party liability, regulatory action, or costs (including reasonable legal costs)** arising from any teaching delivered by the Instructor while any insurance required under clause 7.2 was lapsed, regardless of whether the lapse was known to either party at the time. This indemnity survives termination of this Agreement.

### 7.4 Liability

Each party indemnifies the other against losses arising from their own negligence. The Instructor indemnifies Coach Carter Ltd against any claim arising from the Instructor's conduct, provided that claim is not covered by insurance Coach Carter Ltd is required to maintain under this Agreement.

## 8. Data and confidentiality

### 8.1 Data ownership

All learner data captured on or via the Platform — including bookings, progress notes, competency tracking, theory test data, payment history, and contact details — **is the property of Coach Carter Ltd**. The Instructor has access to this data only via the Platform during the Term and only insofar as it relates to learners they teach.

### 8.2 Post-termination data

On termination:

- The Instructor's access to the Platform is revoked.
- The Instructor must not export, copy, or retain the learner list, progress notes, or contact details.

#### E.1 Purpose and consideration

Under this Part E, the Instructor permits Coach Carter Ltd to use dashcam footage from the Instructor's lessons (subject to learner consent obtained separately via the Platform) for the production and publication of social-media content. In exchange, Coach Carter Ltd pays the Instructor a Content Facilitation Fee, which is **netted against the Weekly Fee in the Instructor's payout calculation** under clause 4. This Part E does not reduce the Weekly Fee; it is an independent fee paid by Coach Carter Ltd to the Instructor and recorded as a separate line on the Instructor's weekly statement.

*[NOTE FOR SOLICITOR]* The fee is structured as a separate facilitation fee netted against the Weekly Fee, rather than a discount on the Weekly Fee, deliberately — so that termination of this Part E does not ambiguously revert the Weekly Fee. Confirm this structure achieves the intended treatment for accounting and contract purposes.

#### E.2 The Instructor's facilitation duties

In consideration of the Content Facilitation Fee, the Instructor shall:

1. Operate dashcam-equipped vehicles on every lesson without modification, in accordance with the dashcam Legitimate Interests Assessment held by Coach Carter Ltd.

2. Provide a brief verbal explanation of the social-media content-consent option to learners during their first lesson, in a tone that neither pressures nor discourages the learner's decision.

3. Not separately publish, share, or distribute any footage from CCL vehicles outside the agreed CCL content pipeline. Coach Carter Ltd may, on the Instructor's written request, license back specific footage for the Instructor's own non-commercial portfolio use, at Coach Carter Ltd's discretion.

#### E.3 Coach Carter Ltd's licence to use footage

Subject to clause E.4 (learner consent) and clause E.5 (instructor's appearance), Coach Carter Ltd may use footage captured under this Arrangement to:

- Edit footage (including but not limited to: cropping, slow-motion, captions, voiceover, music, splicing with other footage, and inclusion in compilations).
- Publish edited footage on the following platforms: TikTok, Instagram (Reels and standard posts), YouTube (Shorts and standard videos), Facebook, X (Twitter), LinkedIn, Snapchat, the CoachCarter website at coachcarter.uk, and paid advertising on Meta and Google.
- Retain all revenue, monetisation, and commercial benefit derived from publication on those platforms (including but not limited to platform creator funds, ad revenue, and any direct sponsorship or branded-content arrangement). The Content Facilitation Fee under E.1 is the Instructor's full and only compensation for participation in this Arrangement.

Adding platforms beyond the list above requires re-consent from each affected learner (because learner consent under E.4 is platform-specific) and is not unilaterally available to Coach Carter Ltd.

#### E.4 Learner consent — gating condition

Coach Carter Ltd shall not edit, publish, or otherwise externally use any footage in which a learner appears unless that learner has granted active social-media-publication consent via the Platform's dedicated consent flow. Learner consent is:

- **Specific** — limited to the platforms listed in E.3 and the editing scope described.
- **Adults-only at launch** — not available to learners under 18.
- **Withdrawable at any time** — withdrawal triggers the takedown process under E.6.
- **Independent of pricing or service** — refusing or withdrawing consent has no impact on lesson availability, lesson rate, or any aspect of the tuition service, other than the discrete 5% lesson discount tied to the consent itself (which is discontinued on withdrawal).

#### E.5 Instructor's appearance and post-publication takedown right

The Instructor licenses their own appearance (image, voice, identifiable likeness) in footage to Coach Carter Ltd for the duration of this Arrangement. The licence covers all uses in clause E.3.

The Instructor does **not** have a per-clip approval right during the editing pipeline. Coach Carter Ltd is the sole gatekeeper for which clips are edited and published.

After publication, the Instructor may request that any specific published clip be taken down. Coach Carter Ltd shall, on receipt of such a request:

- Use reasonable efforts to remove the clip from the platforms listed in E.3 within fourteen (14) days.
- Acknowledge that reasonable-efforts obligation does not extend to third-party reshares, downloads, or archive copies that may persist outside the platforms listed in E.3.
- Not require the Instructor to give a reason for the request, and not unreasonably refuse the request.

This per-clip takedown right is in addition to, and survives, termination under E.7.

#### E.6 Learner-initiated takedown

Where a learner withdraws their social-media-publication consent under E.4, Coach Carter Ltd shall, within fourteen (14) days:

- Remove all clips featuring that learner from the platforms listed in E.3, using reasonable efforts.
- Update internal records to reflect the withdrawn status.

The Instructor is not required to be involved in learner-initiated takedown processing. Coach Carter Ltd, as the data controller for the published footage, is solely responsible for fulfilling withdrawal requests.
