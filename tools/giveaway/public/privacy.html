This preview uses fictional information stored on this laptop. It does not send email or text messages, run analytics, or subscribe anyone to marketing. It uses necessary session cookies to keep the private invitation and fictional admin session available.

Draft campaign notice

CoachCarter will use nomination and application details to administer this giveaway, invite nominees and review applications. The nominee will see the nominator’s name, but not their contact details or nomination paragraph. Campaign reviewers can see the nomination and linked application.

Only nominate someone if you have their permission to provide their contact details. Please avoid unnecessary sensitive information in your nomination or application. You can leave all learning-preference and marketing boxes unchecked.

Learning preferences are expressions of interest. Filming and publication require a separate agreement. Email and text marketing permissions are separate from entry and from each other, and can be withdrawn.

Before publication

The final notice must confirm the controller’s contact details, purposes and lawful bases, approved retention period, processors, rights-request route and how non-account applicants can request access, correction or erasure. It must link to the existing website privacy policy.

Proposed retention for approval: delete unsuccessful nominations and applications 90 days after the campaign closes. Retain only the minimum separately agreed winner information needed to deliver the prize. Marketing evidence and suppression records need their own approved retention rules.

This proposed policy is not active. The local preview has no automatic retention schedule and must contain fictional data only.
