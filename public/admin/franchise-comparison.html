How the numbers are worked out

  • Based on 52 weeks a year
  • Take-home = your gross income, minus the weekly fee, insurance, maintenance, and the time you spend on admin
  • Admin time is valued at your hourly rate - those are hours you could be teaching
  • The weekly fee can never exceed what you've earned that week - you'll never owe money on a quiet week
  • Petrol is left out - it's roughly the same either way
  • The numbers above are pure cash. They don't put a price on the brand, the booking platform, the learner funnel, or the support you'd get

Numbers are yours to play with. Adjust anything that doesn't reflect your situation - the comparison is only as honest as the inputs.
