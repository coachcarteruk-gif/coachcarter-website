How the maths works

  • 50 working weeks per year (2 weeks off)
  • Take-home = gross − franchise fee − insurance − maintenance − admin time cost
  • Admin time cost = admin hrs/wk × lesson price × 50 weeks. Time is real money even if it doesn't leave the bank account
  • Franchise fee is capped at weekly gross (instructor never goes negative on a quiet week)
  • Petrol is left out — same in both scenarios
  • Doesn't price in non-cash benefits: brand, AI tools, platform features, compliance, support
