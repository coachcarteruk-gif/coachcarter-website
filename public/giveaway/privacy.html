Your information

A little clarity.

This notice explains how information is used for Fraser’s free driving lessons offer. Please share it with your nominee before submitting their details. Updated 9 October 2026.

Who looks after your information

Coach Carter Ltd, trading as CoachCarter, is the controller identified in our website privacy policy. Fraser handles campaign privacy questions and requests. Email fraser@coachcarter.uk. You do not need a website account to contact us.

What we collect and why

From the nominator, we collect their name, email and mobile number, the nominee’s name and contact details, the nomination paragraph, permission confirmation, optional relationship and their own optional email/text marketing choices. We use these to administer the nomination and automatically email the nominee their private application link.

The nominee supplies their application answers, confirms their contact details, and can optionally tell us about learning preferences and choose email or text marketing. We record submissions, consent choices and subsequent changes to administer the giveaway and respect those choices.

We receive the nominee’s initial details from their nominator. After the nominator confirms permission to provide their details so we can invite them to apply, we automatically email the nominee their private application link. They see the nominator’s name, but not the nominator’s contact details or private nomination paragraph. Authorised campaign reviewers can see the linked nomination and application. Please avoid unnecessary health or other sensitive information.

Where information is stored

Original submissions, consent evidence and privacy records are stored in the CoachCarter database on Neon. Vercel hosts the website and API, and Resend delivers invitation emails. Private application links protect access to the application. The nominator also receives a nomination confirmation email containing the nominee’s name, private application link and deadline. The same link appears on the submission confirmation screen so they can forward it directly. Anyone with that link can access the application, so it should be shared only with the nominee.

Necessary cookies protect application access. Analytics and session replay are disabled on giveaway pages.

Our service providers may process information outside the UK. Their data-processing terms describe the safeguards for international transfers, including standard contractual clauses and the UK addendum where applicable: Neon, Vercel and Resend. Contact Fraser for information about the safeguards applicable to your data.

Your optional choices

You can leave all learning-preference and marketing boxes unchecked. Nominating someone alone does not subscribe either person to marketing. Nominators can choose CoachCarter offers and future nomination opportunities; nominees make their own choices for relevant offers. Email and text marketing require separate choices. Learning preferences are expressions of interest; filming or publication requires a separate agreement.

You can withdraw your optional marketing permissions by emailing us at fraser@coachcarter.uk. Nominees can also use their private application link. This stops future giveaway marketing; it does not cancel your nomination or application. We keep a minimal suppression record so a later nomination does not accidentally subscribe you again.

Why we may use your information

We rely on legitimate interests to administer this offer, assess nominations and applications, prevent duplicate entries and misuse, and respond to requests. We limit the information collected and ask you to avoid sensitive details. You can object to this processing by contacting Fraser.

Optional email and text marketing rely on your separate consent. The nomination permission confirmation covers providing details for the private application invitation; it does not subscribe the nominee to further promotional emails. The application invitation is sent automatically after nomination, and marketing choices remain separate.

How long we keep it

Unsuccessful nominations and application answers will be deleted 90 days after the campaign closes. We will keep the winner’s necessary details while providing the free lessons. Promotional contact details and consent records are managed separately from nomination paragraphs and application answers.

Retention is reviewed manually. After the winner’s lessons finish, giveaway details are kept only as needed to resolve outstanding queries or meet record-keeping obligations; any separate lesson records follow the website privacy policy. We review promotional contacts and consent evidence when unsuccessful entries are deleted, retaining them only while there is a continuing purpose and the permission remains valid. After an opt-out, we retain only the minimum evidence needed to honour that choice and demonstrate how consent was handled. You can ask Fraser about a record or request deletion at any time.

Access, corrections and other requests

Email fraser@coachcarter.uk to ask for access, correction, deletion, restriction or to object to processing. We may need to verify your identity and will explain any information we must retain. A nominee’s access request does not reveal someone else’s private nomination paragraph or contact details.

You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office. Read the website privacy policy alongside this campaign notice and the giveaway terms.
