Your information

A little clarity.

Draft for review. This website is not publicly open; nominations have already been invited on Facebook. Only fictional information should be entered during testing. Retention periods, the campaign’s lawful bases and provider arrangements still need approval before publication.

Who looks after your information

Coach Carter Ltd, trading as CoachCarter, is the controller identified in our website privacy policy. For campaign privacy questions or requests, email fraser@coachcarter.uk. You do not need a website account to contact us.

What we collect and why

From the nominator, we collect their name, email and mobile number, the nominee’s name and contact details, the nomination paragraph, permission confirmation and optional relationship. We use these to administer the nomination and invite the nominee to apply.

The nominee supplies their application answers, confirms their contact details, and can optionally tell us about learning preferences and choose email or text marketing. We record submissions, consent choices and subsequent changes to administer the giveaway and respect those choices.

Nominees receive their invitation because someone supplied their details with permission. They see the nominator’s name, but not the nominator’s contact details or private nomination paragraph. Authorised campaign reviewers can see the linked nomination and application. Please avoid unnecessary health or other sensitive information.

Where information is stored

Original submissions, consent evidence and privacy records are stored in the CoachCarter database on Neon. Selected contact details and nomination status are copied to HighLevel for staff administration. Vercel hosts the website and API, and Resend delivers invitation emails. Private application links protect access to the application.

Necessary cookies protect application access. Analytics and session replay are disabled on giveaway pages.

Before launch: confirm each provider’s contractual safeguards, processing locations and any international-transfer arrangements for this campaign.

Your optional choices

You can leave all learning-preference and marketing boxes unchecked. Nominating someone does not subscribe either person to marketing. Email and text marketing require separate choices. Learning preferences are expressions of interest; filming or publication requires a separate agreement.

You can withdraw your optional marketing permissions using your private application link or by emailing us. This stops future giveaway marketing; it does not cancel your application. We keep a minimal suppression record so a later nomination does not accidentally subscribe you again.

Lawful bases — for approval

The proposed basis for running and reviewing the campaign is legitimate interests, subject to a documented assessment. Optional email and text marketing would rely on consent. Before launch, we must separately confirm the basis and electronic-communications rules for inviting someone whose details came from a nominator. The permission checkbox alone does not settle that assessment.

How long we keep it — for approval

The proposal is to delete unsuccessful nominations and applications 90 days after the campaign closes. Minimum winner information would be retained to deliver the prize. Exact periods for winner records, consent evidence and suppression records still need approval. Retention automation is not active during this preview.

Access, corrections and other requests

Email fraser@coachcarter.uk to ask for access, correction, deletion, restriction or to object to processing. We may need to verify your identity and will explain any information we must retain. A nominee’s access request does not reveal someone else’s private nomination paragraph or contact details.

You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office. Read the website privacy policy alongside this campaign notice and the draft giveaway terms.
