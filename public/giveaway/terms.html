The main prize

One winner receives one free one-hour automatic driving lesson each week until they pass their practical driving test. Use of the car for 1.5 hours on the day of every practical test attempt is included. The learner is responsible for purchasing their test bookings. The weekly lessons are subject to the lesson cancellation conditions below.

Pass price

The Lifelong Learner Pass is priced at £2,000. The giveaway winner receives the Pass free of charge. This is the price of the Pass, not a cash balance or a limit on the number of weekly lessons included under these terms.

Who can apply

For the main prize, the nominee must live in postcode district RG1, RG2, RG5 or RG6, be aged 17 or over, hold a valid provisional driving licence and not be disqualified from driving. These are postcode districts: for example, RG1 does not include RG10.

People outside these four postcode districts may submit their details, but they are not eligible for the main-prize award. No runner-up prizes are advertised as part of this giveaway. Optional marketing permissions are separate from entry and do not affect anyone’s chance of winning.

To confirm: how residence and licence eligibility for the main prize will be checked.

How to enter

One entry per person. Being nominated by more than one person does not give your application extra weight.

Ask someone’s permission before providing their contact details through the nomination form. We email the nominee a private application link. They must complete their application to be considered; a nomination alone is not a completed entry.

Applications close on Sunday 11 October 2026 at 10pm UK time (Europe/London). Nominate early enough for the person to receive the invitation and complete their application.

Optional learning preferences and email or text marketing choices are separate from applying. Expressing interest in filmed lessons is not permission to film or publish footage; that would require a separate agreement.

Choosing and contacting the winner

One person will receive the Pass following a review of eligible nominations and applications. We will give equal importance to the difference driving would make to your everyday life and the barriers these free lessons could help you overcome. Final judging arrangements are being confirmed.

Writing ability, number of nominations, marketing choices and willingness to be filmed will not influence the decision. Independent judging arrangements must be confirmed before this draft is used.

The planned winner selection date is Monday 12 October 2026. We will contact the winner by email and phone on the same day. The winner must respond within five calendar days (120 hours) of that notification. If the winner does not respond within that period, a replacement will need to be selected manually. The judged replacement procedure is to be confirmed.

Main prize: before your lessons begin

If selected, you must have passed your theory test and have a practical driving test booked in Reading before your free weekly lessons can begin. CoachCarter can offer advice on passing your theory test and arranging your practical test booking.

You can apply before you have passed your theory test or booked your practical test. These requirements must be met before lessons start.

Arranging lessons and the test

The included 1.5 hours of test-day car use covers every attempt. The learner pays for their test bookings.

Cancelling your weekly lessons

If you need to cancel one of your free weekly lessons, you must give your instructor at least 48 hours’ notice before the scheduled start time so they can make rearrangements.

When you cancel a lesson, it is at the instructor’s discretion whether that lesson is carried forward into a future week. Carrying it forward is not automatic.

If your instructor cannot attend and cancels a lesson, the cancelled lesson hours will be carried forward and added to future lessons.

If you cancel three of your free weekly lessons with less than 48 hours’ notice, your free weekly lessons will end. Instructor cancellations do not count towards these three occasions.
