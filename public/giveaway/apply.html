A skill that opens doors

More freedom. More possibilities.

The journey to work. Visiting the people you love. Saying yes to an opportunity without wondering how you’ll get there.

Learning to drive can make everyday life feel more like your own. This Pass gives one learner regular support behind the wheel, helping them build confidence and work towards passing their practical test — one week at a time.

What the Pass gives you

01

A lesson every week

One free one-hour automatic driving lesson each week until you pass your practical driving test, subject to the weekly lesson conditions.

02

Support on test day

Use of the car for 1.5 hours on the day of every practical test attempt. You pay for your test bookings with DVSA.

03

Help with the next steps

Advice on preparing for your theory test and arranging your practical test booking in Reading.

These free lessons are personally offered by Fraser. CoachCarter’s website is used to manage nominations and applications.

Weekly lesson cancellations

Give your instructor at least 48 hours’ notice. If you cancel three free weekly lessons with less than 48 hours’ notice, your free weekly lessons will end.

Carrying a learner-cancelled lesson forward is at the instructor’s discretion. Instructor-cancelled hours are carried forward and do not count towards the three late cancellations.
