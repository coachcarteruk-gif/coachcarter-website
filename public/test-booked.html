Before you choose a time

Is it really free?

One hour, no payment card and no obligation to continue. Existing trial eligibility and available slots apply.

Do I need a test booked?

No. We start from where you are, whether or not you have a date.

My test is soon — can you get me ready?

We can discuss your driving and realistic options. One lesson cannot promise a pass or readiness by your date; sometimes more time or practice will be appropriate.

Can I use your car for my test?

The trial does not confirm test-day instructor or car availability. Discuss this separately with your instructor.

Who teaches me, and where?

The instructor assigned to your selected available slot. Check the pickup and transmission details when choosing your lesson.

What if my test changes?

Update Driving Test in your learner account, or tell your instructor. This does not automatically change a lesson or test-day arrangement.

Must I watch a video or buy a package?

No to both. Booking is available immediately. Further lessons can be discussed after you have experienced the teaching.
