Good to know

Before you buy Flexible Hours

Pay by Bank only. You will leave CoachCarter briefly to approve the payment with your bank.

We will confirm your payment. This is normally quick, but please allow up to 24 hours. If it is still pending, do not pay again—contact us and we will check it.

Your hours are flexible. They do not expire and can be used with any available CoachCarter instructor, but they cannot be transferred to another learner.

Your cancellation rights still apply. You have 14 days to cancel. Once you ask for immediate access, hours you use or lose through a late cancellation may be deducted; unused hours are refunded at the rate paid.

Existing Lesson Credit is unchanged. Any Lesson Credit already on your account remains available under its current rules.
